How it works

Three steps. About ten seconds of your time.

  1. 1

    Tell it who and what

    Search a business, pick one or several contacts from your phone, or type any number. Say what you need in plain words: “reschedule my dentist to next week.”

  2. 2

    RingVo makes the call

    Now, or at the time you schedule. It introduces itself as your AI assistant, navigates the menu, waits on hold, and talks to the agent. Follow along live in the app.

  3. 3

    You get the result

    A short summary of what happened: the new appointment time, the credit applied, the confirmation number. By notification, SMS or WhatsApp.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does the business know it's an AI?

Yes. RingVo always introduces itself as an AI assistant calling on your behalf, at the start of every call.

What can RingVo say for me?

What you tell it, plus the profile details you choose to save, like your name, account number or callback number. It only shares them when the business asks, and it won't agree to anything outside the limits you set.

What happens when it needs a decision?

You get a notification (or a text) with the question and simple answers. Reply from your lock screen and the call continues. If you don't answer in time, RingVo follows your default: ask, skip, or end the call.

Can I call several people at once?

Yes. In New call, choose Contacts, tick up to 10 people, and RingVo makes a separate call to each with the same task, now or at a time you pick.

How does missed-call answering work?

You get a RingVo phone number and forward unanswered calls to it from your carrier settings (the app walks you through it). RingVo takes messages, screens sales calls and ends spam calls.

Where is RingVo available?

In the United States, on iPhone and Android. It calls US and Canadian numbers.

How much does it cost?

Your first 5 calls are free. After that it's $9.99 a month for unlimited calls. Cancel any time.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings › Account › Delete my account. Your profile, calls, recordings and sign-in are permanently deleted.

Ringo Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026. Draft: have counsel review before public launch.

Ringo is an AI phone assistant. It calls businesses for you and answers calls you miss. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your name and email from Google or Apple sign-in. With Apple's "Hide My Email" we only see the relay address.
  • Profile: the mobile number you verify by text message, and the address or ZIP code you enter (used to find businesses near you).
  • Calls you ask Ringo to make: who to call, your goal and limits, and the result. During the call we keep a transcript, an AI summary and, where the call is recorded, the audio.
  • Calls Ringo answers for you: the caller's number, the message they leave, a transcript and a summary.
  • Contacts: only the single contact you pick. Ringo never uploads your address book.
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can tell you when a call needs you.
  • Feedback: star ratings and comments you send from the app.
  • Billing: if you subscribe, Stripe handles your card. We only keep a Stripe customer ID and your subscription status, never your card number.
  • Calendar (optional): if you connect Google Calendar, Ringo checks when you're free and adds bookings it makes. We store an encrypted access token, not your calendar.

We don't collect your location from your device, we don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your personal information for advertising.

How we use it

Only to provide Ringo: placing and answering calls, showing you live progress, asking you questions mid-call, sending notifications, preventing abuse and keeping the service secure.

Who processes it for us

  • Amazon Web Services hosts our servers and database (United States).
  • Retell AI runs the voice agent that speaks on calls, including speech-to-text and language models.
  • Twilio carries the phone calls and sends verification texts, and SMS or WhatsApp questions if you choose them.
  • Stripe processes subscription payments.
  • Google provides sign-in and business search. Only your search text and ZIP code are sent, not your identity.
  • Apple provides sign-in and push notifications.

Text messages (SMS and WhatsApp)

If you choose "Text me" in Settings, Ringo texts your verified mobile number only during calls you started, to ask a question it needs answered. Message frequency varies with your calls. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time, or HELP for help; you can also switch back to in-app questions in Settings. Ringo deletes these messages from Twilio shortly after you answer.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text-message opt-in with third parties or affiliates for marketing. Your number is shared only with the providers above to deliver the messages.

Calls, recording and AI disclosure

At the start of every call Ringo says it is an AI assistant calling on your behalf and that the call may be recorded. You are responsible for only asking Ringo to make lawful calls.

Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AWS KMS). Sign-in tokens on your phone are kept in the iOS Keychain, only on that device. Ringo never stores your account numbers, passwords or PINs. If a business asks for one, Ringo asks you in the app during the call.

Retention

Call history and transcripts are kept until you delete your account. Recordings are deleted after 90 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time in Settings, then Account, then Delete my account. This permanently deletes your profile, call history, messages, recordings and sign-in.
  • Turn off notifications or contacts access in iOS Settings.
  • California and other residents can ask us what we hold or ask us to delete it by emailing us.

Children

Ringo is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

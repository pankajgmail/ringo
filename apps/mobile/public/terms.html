Ringvo Terms of Use

Effective November 24, 2026. Draft: have counsel review before public launch.

The service

Ringvo places and answers phone calls for you using an AI voice assistant. Results depend on the businesses and people it talks to, and we can't guarantee any outcome.

Your responsibilities

  • Only ask Ringvo to call businesses or people you have a legitimate reason to contact, and only for yourself.
  • No harassment, spam, robocalling, threats, fraud, emergency services (911) or impersonating someone else.
  • Keep your sign-in secure. You're responsible for calls placed from your account.

AI and recording

Ringvo always says it's an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. Some places require everyone on a call to consent to recording. If the other side doesn't consent, Ringvo ends the call.

Plans and payment

New accounts include 5 free outbound calls. After that, Ringvo monthly costs $9.99 per month (plus any tax), billed by Stripe. It renews automatically each month until you cancel. Cancel anytime in Settings, then Plan, then Manage subscription; you keep access until the end of the month you paid for. We don't give refunds for partial months except where the law requires. Answering missed calls stays free. We'll tell you before we change the price.

Text messages

If you turn on SMS or WhatsApp questions, Ringvo texts you only during calls you started. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Limits

We may limit call volume, length and spending, and suspend accounts that misuse the service.

Liability

Ringvo is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses or for what third parties agree to or do on a call.

Ending

You can delete your account at any time in the app. We may end the service with notice.

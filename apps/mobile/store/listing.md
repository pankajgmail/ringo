**Description** (4000)
Never wait on hold again.

Ringvo is your AI phone assistant. Tell it who to call and what you need, like "reschedule my dentist appointment to next week" or "ask the cable company to waive the late fee." Ringvo places the call, sits through the hold music and phone menus, and talks to the business for you.

WHEN IT NEEDS YOU, IT ASKS
If the business offers a choice, like a different time slot or a price, Ringvo sends you a notification or a text. Reply right from your lock screen and Ringvo carries on. You stay in control without being stuck on the phone.

ANSWERS THE CALLS YOU MISS
Forward unanswered calls to your Ringvo line. Ringvo greets callers as your assistant, takes a message with a callback number, and sends you a summary.
• Robocalls and scams get a polite "not interested" and the call ends.
• Sales calls get asked what you'd get, what it costs, and whether there's a deadline, so you can decide later.
• Every call is saved in your call history with a short summary.

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE
• Pick a contact or type any business number
• Live status while your call is happening
• Results by notification, SMS or WhatsApp
• Appointments you book can go straight onto your Google Calendar
• Fill in your profile once so Ringvo can share your name, account details and callback number when a business asks

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Ringvo reads contacts only when you pick one. It doesn't track you or sell your data, and you can delete your account and data at any time from Settings.

PRICING
Your first 5 calls are free. After that, Ringvo is $9.99 a month for unlimited calls. Cancel any time.

Questions or feedback? Tap the star in the app or email support@ringo.app.

**What's New** (version 1.1.0)
Meet Ringvo. Your AI assistant now answers missed calls, screens sales calls and blocks spam, can text you by SMS or WhatsApp, and adds booked appointments to Google Calendar. You can also rate the app and send us feedback in a couple of taps.

**App Review notes**
Sign in with Apple or Google. A demo account isn't needed: new accounts get 5 free calls. To test, tap New call, pick any business number (for example a store's public line), type a request, and watch the live status. Ringvo identifies itself as an AI assistant at the start of every call.
